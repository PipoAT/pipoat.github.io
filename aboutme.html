About Me

Hello! I am Andrew T. Pipo, an Embedded Systems Engineer, Co-op Program Coordinator, and IT Support professional at ATech Training. I am based in Springfield, Ohio.

I earned a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from the University of Cincinnati's College of Engineering and Applied Science, along with a minor in Information Technology and a certificate in Software Engineering.

At ATech Training, I develop embedded C code for cabinet trainers, support Python-based software trainers, design PCBs in KiCad, and build automation jigs for internal testing. I oversee co-op projects, lead recruitment for the co-op program, help administer company IT, and consult with the marketing team on the company website.

I am also helping modernize legacy hardware and software. This includes designing daughterboards to extend the life of existing products, documenting legacy assembly behavior with task and state diagrams, and migrating that software to C. I organize project work in Azure DevOps and build internal tools to make design and code reviews more efficient.

Earlier at ATech Training, I completed three co-op rotations focused on embedded engineering, Android and web applications, graphical interfaces, serial communications, testing, and automation. I also served as a Lead Peer-Teaching Assistant for the University of Cincinnati's ENED 1100 and ENED 1120 courses, supporting course development and logistics while leading a three-person teaching assistant team.

My technical skills include C, C++, Python, PCB design, process automation, and IT, along with experience in C#, JavaScript, TypeScript, SQL, Kotlin, LabVIEW, and web development. Recent professional development includes Anthropic's AI Fluency Framework and Claude 101 certificates. Before my engineering roles, I worked in customer service at Accuserve Solutions and Staples.

Outside of work, I volunteer as a mentor with Adopt A Class, helping fifth-grade students explore career opportunities through activities and project work.
